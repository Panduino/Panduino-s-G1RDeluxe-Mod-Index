# Balanced EXP Share

Adds a party-wide catch-up EXP system to FireRed and LeafGreen.

Pokémon that participate in battle keep their normal EXP. Healthy nonparticipants receive reduced EXP based on their level relative to the defeated Pokémon, helping weaker party members catch up without rapidly pushing the whole team above the game's level curve.

Passive EXP does not grant EVs and is not boosted by Lucky Egg or the traded-Pokémon bonus.
